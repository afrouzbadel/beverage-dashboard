# Beverage Sales Dashboard

**One-liner:** Interactive dashboard analyzing beverage sales, unit volumes and operating profit across brands and retail channels.

---

## Project 
**Data (sample):**

| Beverage Brand | Total Sales | Units Sold | Price per Unit | Operating Margin | Operating Profit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Fanta | 476,713 | 957,500 | 124.35 | 95.65 | 175,240 |
| Powerade | 538,338 | 919,250 | 148.80 | 94.35 | 199,421 |
| Sprite | 543,925 | 1,092,250 | 123.65 | 92.15 | 192,982 |
| Diet Coke | 681,113 | 1,315,250 | 129.80 | 90.95 | 232,207 |
| Dasani Water | 757,150 | 1,209,000 | 161.15 | 100.30 | 288,244 |
| Coca-Cola | 895,838 | 1,645,500 | 139.95 | 102.40 | 356,994 |
| **Total** | **3,893,075** | **7,138,750** | **827.70** | **575.80** | **1,445,088** |

---

## Key insights (summary)
- **Coca-Cola** shows the highest Total Sales and Operating Profit — this brand is the top contributor to profit.  
- **Dasani Water** has the highest price per unit but also a strong margin, yielding high operating profit.  
- Units sold vs price trade-off visible: some high-unit low-price SKUs (e.g., Sprite) still contribute sizeable revenue.  
- Top retail channels by contribution: Walmart, CVS, Costco, Target (Walmart leads with ~2.05M in sales).
